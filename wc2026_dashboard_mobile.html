How it works & how to drive it

Model = engine v2 (Fable recut): dynamic Elo + rolling form + incremental attack/defence ratings + Elo momentum, recency-weighted (12-year half-life) → blended logistic / twin-Poisson outcomes with a fitted per-class calibration (avg T=0.98) and Dixon-Coles-corrected twin-Poisson scorelines (ρ fitted by maximum likelihood, =-0.05). Selected by backtest against the previous engine: more SuperBru points on 5 past tournaments (292.0 vs 278.0 under the official scoring rules — also beating the "2-1 favourite" heuristic, which the old engine never did), better calibration, more exact scores; gradient boosting and xG features were tested and rejected. Scoring implements the official SuperBru rules: 3 exact / 1.5 close / 1 result, close = 1 goal out or 2 goals out with the correct goal difference; knockout picks are optimised on the post-extra-time score (a shootout is scored as a draw).
